We’re proud to share that OWASP is once again an official mentoring organization for Google Summer of Code (GSoC) 2025 —and this year, we’ve secured 15 contributor slots across some of the most impactful open-source security projects in the world. This milestone reinforces OWASP’s ongoing commitment to open-source innovation and highlights the strength of our global community of mentors and contributors, as well as our commitment to education-by-doing with the GSoC program. 🔧 GSoC 2025: What’s on Deck?
