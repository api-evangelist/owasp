OWASP has forged a coalition between the global security community and formal standardization bodies on AI. The ‘AI Exchange’ Project is now driving the creation of AI security standards that protect people and businesses while still allowing innovation to thrive. By establishing an official liaison partnership with international standardization organizations, the Open Worldwide Application Security Project (OWASP) has opened the door for its community of 8,500 security professionals to contribute directly to critical standards.
