For community clarity following several recent announcements by ISSA and Richard Greenberg , OWASP confirms that Richard Greenberg’s membership and participation in all OWASP activities was terminated in August 2023. This followed a formal independent review process conducted in accordance with OWASP’s governing documents and Code of Conduct. All chapter leadership roles and associated privileges ceased at that time.
